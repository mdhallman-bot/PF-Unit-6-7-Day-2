2. Aaliyah’s nursing cost comparison

Aaliyah is choosing between a two-year community-college associate degree nursing pathway and a four-year university bachelor's-degree pathway. Both examples assume appropriate accredited nursing programs and licensure requirements. These are fictional classroom estimates to practice comparing costs, not real school prices or guaranteed aid offers.

Important: These figures exclude housing, food, and personal living expenses to make the comparison manageable. That limitation matters! Students should identify additional costs in their recommendation. The two pathways also differ in length and qualifications, which will matter in Day 3.

New information: Path B has a higher advertised financial aid total, but the student loan offered is not a discount. Also, Aaliyah may be able to complete a bachelor's later while working as an RN, depending on transfer agreements and program rules.

New challenge · Can Aaliyah make the university pathway work?

Path B has a $33,000 funding gap after non-loan resources. The current loan offer covers at most $18,000, leaving $15,000 unaccounted for. This does not mean the university is impossible. Aaliyah needs a more complete funding plan.

Important: The figures cover the full four-year program, not one academic year. Funding availability, grants and borrowing limits may change by year. A payment plan can change when bills are due but does not reduce total cost. Additional borrowing can close an arithmetic gap but may create serious repayment risks.

Other possibilities to investigate: lower expenses, education tax benefits where eligible, school payment schedules, transfer arrangements, or an associate degree followed by an RN-to-BSN option. Verify eligibility with the institution; don't assume private loans or federal loans are automatically available.

STOP — Wait for class discussion. Compare Aaliyah’s two paths. How is a loan different from a discount?

PowerPoint notes · Good AI research (Slides 18–19)

A. Start with your career — discover the available routes

Open MagicSchool AI — Student Join Link ↗

First question for MagicSchool: Before looking for costs or specific programs, find out which routes can actually lead to your career. Some careers require a degree and license; others offer an apprenticeship, short-term training, direct employment, or several options. Do not assume college is required.

Ask MagicSchool to distinguish required qualifications from optional training, and to point you to an official career, licensing, training, or employer source. If you have more than one route, you may choose the one you want to investigate.

B. Build YOUR regional AI research prompt

Use the route you chose above. Include career, your chosen training or employment route, region, two real local opportunities, typical low–high expense ranges, why estimates vary, and official direct source links with dates. Ask for starting pay and a separately labeled occupational median. If AI cannot verify a number, it must label it as unverified. You may change jobs or cities.
