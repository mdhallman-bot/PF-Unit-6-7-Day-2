New information: Path B has a higher advertised financial aid total, but the student loan offered is not a discount. Also, Aaliyah may be able to complete a bachelor's later while working as an RN, depending on transfer agreements and program rules.

New challenge · Can Aaliyah make the university pathway work?

Path B has a $33,000 funding gap after non-loan resources. The current loan offer covers at most $18,000, leaving $15,000 unaccounted for. This does not mean the university is impossible. Aaliyah needs a more complete funding plan.

Important: The figures cover the full four-year program, not one academic year. Funding availability, grants and borrowing limits may change by year. A payment plan can change when bills are due but does not reduce total cost. Additional borrowing can close an arithmetic gap but may create serious repayment risks.

Enter estimates and calculate. The target is $15,000; these choices are not guaranteed funding.

Other possibilities to investigate: lower expenses, education tax benefits where eligible, school payment schedules, transfer arrangements, or an associate degree followed by an RN-to-BSN option. Verify eligibility with the institution; don't assume private loans or federal loans are automatically available.

47–59 minutes · Name the ideas + official site investigation

5. How do you actually pay?

You just compared Aaliyah's community-college and university nursing pathways. The university costs more, and a larger aid offer does not automatically make it less expensive. Now name the kinds of costs and financial resources you found, then investigate the actual FAFSA and CASFA websites. Hover or tap underlined words for definitions.

Costs

Direct costs are billed or required for training. Indirect costs include transportation and living expenses. Opportunity cost includes time or earnings you give up.

Cost of attendance is broader than tuition; net price subtracts grants and scholarships, not loans.

Funding

A scholarship or grant generally lowers the price without repayment. Employer assistance can help cover eligible costs. Student loans create debt. Work-study is earned through work.

Official aid websites — research only. Do not start an application, sign in, or enter your name, immigration status, family income, or other private information. Investigate the FAFSA and the CASFA using the official pages linked below.
